# Corporate Action Notice — Partial Tender Offer
**Security:** Oldfield Capital plc (GB0001271211) · **Market:** GB · **Event ref:** CAE-002706

## Key dates
Announcement 2025-08-19 · Response deadline 2025-08-21 · Payment 2025-08-27 · Notice window 2 business days

## Terms
The offeror is offering to purchase **up to 30% of the issued share capital** for cash at a premium to the undisturbed share price.

## Scaling
If tenders exceed 30% of the issued share capital, **acceptances will be scaled back pro rata**, and shares not purchased are returned to holders.

## Conditions and withdrawal
The offer is conditional on regulatory clearance. Tendered shares may be withdrawn until the offer is declared unconditional.

## Default if no instruction
Holders who do not tender retain their shares; no consideration is paid.
