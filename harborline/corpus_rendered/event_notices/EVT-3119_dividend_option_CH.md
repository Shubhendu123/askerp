# Corporate Action Notice — Dividend Option
**Security:** Crestfall Financial AG (CH0001135794) · **Market:** CH · **Event ref:** CAE-003119

## Key dates
Announcement 2025-10-03 · Record date 2025-10-03 · Response deadline 2025-10-06 · Payment 2025-10-13 · Notice window 3 business days

## Terms
Dividend of CHF 6.2936 per share payable in **cash** or, at the holder's election, in **new shares**.

## Tax treatment
The cash dividend is subject to Swiss withholding tax at 35%. **New shares issued under the share option are also subject to withholding tax on their value**, which is settled in cash from the holder's account. Holders electing shares should ensure sufficient cash is available.

## Default if no instruction
Holders who do not elect receive **cash**.
