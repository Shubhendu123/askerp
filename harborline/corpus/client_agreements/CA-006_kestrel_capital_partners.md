# Custody Services Agreement — Schedule of Negotiated Terms
## Client: Kestrel Capital Partners (hedge fund, GOLD service tier)

## 1. SLA credit cap
Notwithstanding POL-001 §3, SLA credits for Kestrel are capped at **8%** of the monthly custody fee (not the standard GOLD 12%).

## 2. Overdraft tolerance
Notwithstanding POL-011 §3, Kestrel's overdraft tolerance is **USD 250,000 for up to 2 business days** without charge, reflecting the client's pre-agreed prime brokerage funding arrangements.
