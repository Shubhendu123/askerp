# Custody Services Agreement — Schedule of Negotiated Terms
## Client: Westmark Sovereign Investment Authority (sovereign wealth)

## 1. Reclaim filing window
Notwithstanding the statute-of-limitations deadlines in POL-005 §5, Harborline must file every eligible reclaim for Westmark **within 90 calendar days of the income payment date**. A reclaim filed after 90 days, or not filed, breaches this schedule even if the statute deadline has not passed.

## 2. Corporate action compensation
Harborline compensates Westmark in full for any missed election (no cap), and additionally pays interest at the overnight benchmark rate from the payment date of the event until compensation is paid.

## 3. Reporting
Harborline provides Westmark with a monthly report of all voluntary events, elections made, defaults applied and any missed deadlines, within 5 business days of month end.
