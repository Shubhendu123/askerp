# Custody Services Agreement — Schedule of Negotiated Terms
## Client: Eastgate Capital Partners (hedge fund, PLATINUM service tier)

## 1. Status of this schedule
This schedule forms part of the Custody Services Agreement between Harborline Global Custody and Eastgate Capital Partners. Where this schedule conflicts with Harborline's standard policies, **this schedule prevails** for this client.

## 2. Settlement performance commitment
Notwithstanding POL-001 §2, the monthly settlement-rate commitment for Eastgate is **98.5%** (not the standard PLATINUM 99.0%), reflecting the client's high trading turnover and short-dated holding periods. All other PLATINUM commitments apply unchanged.

## 3. Transaction fees
The 25% hedge-fund premium on transaction fees in POL-010 §4 is **waived** for Eastgate. Transaction fees are charged at the standard developed-market rate.

## 4. Penalty allocation
CSD cash penalties are allocated per POL-002 §5. Penalties arising from Harborline's own instruction-processing errors remain with Harborline and are not passed to the client.

## 5. Review
These terms are reviewed annually at the September service review.
