# Custody Services Agreement — Schedule of Negotiated Terms
## Client: Pinnacle Asset Management (asset manager, PLATINUM service tier)

## 1. Status of this schedule
Where this schedule conflicts with Harborline's standard policies, this schedule prevails for this client.

## 2. Liability for corporate action errors
Notwithstanding POL-004 §8, Harborline's liability to Pinnacle for any single missed corporate action election is **capped at USD 2,000,000 per event**. Losses above the cap are borne by the client, except where the miss results from Harborline's gross negligence or wilful default, in which case the cap does not apply.

## 3. Notification
For voluntary events, Harborline notifies Pinnacle **the same business day as announcement**, irrespective of the PLATINUM window in POL-001 §2, and confirms receipt by telephone for any event with a notice window of 3 business days or fewer.

## 4. Client response deadline
Pinnacle's internal response deadline is two business days before the market deadline (not one, as in POL-004 §4), to allow for the client's own investment committee process.
