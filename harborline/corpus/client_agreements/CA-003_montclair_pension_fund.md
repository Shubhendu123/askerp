# Custody Services Agreement — Schedule of Negotiated Terms
## Client: Montclair Pension Fund (pension fund)

## 1. Tax services mandate
Montclair has executed Harborline's tax services mandate. Harborline **must file every eligible withholding tax reclaim** on the client's behalf (POL-005 §6). Failure to file an eligible reclaim before its statute deadline is a Harborline error, and Harborline compensates the client for the forfeited amount.

## 2. Pension-rate relief
Montclair qualifies for the exempt or reduced pension-fund treaty rate in all treaty markets listed in POL-005 §3. Harborline maintains the client's certificates of residence and renews them annually by 31 January.

## 3. Tender offers
For TENDER_OFFER events, Harborline notifies the client's chief investment officer **by telephone** in addition to standard electronic notification, and records the call. Absence of a recorded call is a breach of this schedule regardless of whether the electronic notice was sent.
