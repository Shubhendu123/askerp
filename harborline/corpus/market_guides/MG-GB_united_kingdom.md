# Market Guide — United Kingdom (CREST)

## 1. Settlement
CSD: CREST. Cycle: T+2, moving to T+1 on 11 October 2027 (REG-002). Penalty regime: CREST, flat daily rate per POL-002 §4. Partial settlement available.
Client instruction cut-off: 17:00 GMT on trade date.

## 2. Corporate actions practice
- **Rights issues** in the UK typically run a short subscription period, and nil-paid rights trade on the exchange during it. A missed take-up instruction means the rights may be sold in the market at the end of the period, or lapse, depending on the issuer's terms.
- **Tender offers** generally carry a closing date and may be extended; acceptances can sometimes be withdrawn until the offer becomes unconditional. Always check the notice for withdrawal rights and conditions.

## 3. Tax
No withholding tax on UK dividends. Reclaim complexity is MEDIUM only because of documentation for REIT property income distributions.
