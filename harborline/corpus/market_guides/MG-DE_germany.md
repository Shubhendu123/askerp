# Market Guide — Germany (Clearstream)

## 1. Settlement
CSD: Clearstream. Cycle: T+2, moving to T+1 on 11 October 2027. CSDR penalties apply. Partial settlement available.
Client instruction cut-off: 17:00 CET on trade date.

## 2. Corporate actions — tender offers
German public takeover offers carry an acceptance period set by the bidder. Where an offer succeeds, German takeover law generally provides an **additional two-week acceptance period** after the results are published, during which shareholders who did not accept may still tender. A tender election missed in the main acceptance period may therefore still be recoverable in the additional period — check the offer document before treating the miss as final.

## 3. Tax
Statutory dividend withholding 26.375% (including solidarity surcharge). Reclaim complexity MEDIUM; enhanced custody-chain reporting requirements apply from 2027. Statute of limitations 36 months.
