# Corporate Action Notice — Rights Issue
**Security:** {{SECURITY_NAME}} ({{ISIN}}) · **Market:** GB · **Event ref:** {{EVENT_REF}}

## Key dates
Announcement {{ANNOUNCEMENT_DATE}} · Record date {{RECORD_DATE}} · Response deadline {{RESPONSE_DEADLINE}} · Payment {{PAYMENT_DATE}} · Notice window {{NOTICE_WINDOW_DAYS}} business days

## Terms
Fully underwritten rights issue of **1 new share for every 4 shares held** at the subscription price set out in the issuer's prospectus. Nil-paid rights are admitted to trading for the duration of the subscription period.

## Options
1. **Take up** in full — pay the subscription price for the new shares.
2. **Sell** all nil-paid rights in the market before the end of the trading period.
3. **Lapse** — take no action.

## Default if no instruction
Rights not taken up are placed with investors by the underwriters at the close of the offer. Any premium over the subscription price, after expenses, is paid to the lapsed holder, provided it exceeds GBP 5. Holders who lapse therefore typically receive less value than those who sell their rights.

## Narrative from the issuer's agent
The subscription period is shorter than usual because the issue was announced alongside an acquisition. Custodians should expect a compressed instruction window.
