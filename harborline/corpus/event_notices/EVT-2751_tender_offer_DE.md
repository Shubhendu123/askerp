## Terms
All-cash offer for **all outstanding shares** at a premium to the three-month volume-weighted average price.

## Minimum acceptance condition
The offer is conditional on a **minimum acceptance level of 50% plus one share** of the issued share capital.

## Additional acceptance period
If the offer succeeds, an **additional acceptance period of two weeks** will run after publication of the results, during which shareholders who did not accept may still tender on the same terms.

## Default if no instruction
Holders who do not accept retain their shares.
